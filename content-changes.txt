SETUP NEEDED TO ACTUALLY RECEIVE LEADS (edit script.js, SITE_CONFIG)
=====================================================================
  formEndpoint   Where emails are sent. Free options: Formspree
                 (formspree.io, create a form, paste the URL), a Make or
                 Zapier webhook, or the MailChimp API via a webhook.
                 Until this is set, emails are saved in the visitor's
                 browser, retried on their next visit, and the visitor is
                 sent straight to Calendly with the email prefilled so the
                 booking still captures it.
  fallbackEmail  Your address. If formEndpoint is empty, the form opens the
                 visitor's mail app addressed to you.
  ga4Id          Google Analytics 4 measurement ID (G-XXXX). Optional.
  metaPixelId    Meta Pixel ID. Optional. Fires a Lead event on form submit
                 and on a completed Calendly booking.

Calendly: the embedded calendar reads the same URL. Add UTM parameters to
links you post (?utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=reel1) and they are
carried into every booking automatically.
  testimonials   Array of real client quotes. The Client stories section
                 stays hidden until it has at least one entry.
