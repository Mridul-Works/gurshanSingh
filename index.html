The roadmap

Five steps. In order. No skipping.

Each step builds trust for the next one. Do them in sequence and your first client shows up before you expect it.

  1. Step 1

    Pick a niche

    Don't have a niche? Don't worry. Finding one takes three questions:

    • Find your "Why"Your purpose or belief. What do you really care about? What difference do you want to make?
    • Find your "How"Which social media platform are you going to use?
    • Find your "What"Coaching — that's what this programme is built around.
    ✦ Niche Finder GPT
    Free custom GPT with keyword prompts to narrow your niche     →

    Be very specific in your niche.

    DON'T"I help people get fit."

    DO"I help men gain muscle."

    DO"I help women lose fat."

  2. Step 2

    Build an audience

    Once you've found your niche, start making content. Divide it into three parts:

    60% 30% 10%
    • 60% · Talking about your nicheteach, show, prove
    • 30% · Salesoffers, CTAs, results
    • 10% · Random stuffyou, your day, your story
    1k followers is enough for your first client. You don't need 100k.
    ICP By now you'll know your Ideal Customer Profile — e.g. men aged 20–40 who want muscle with only 30 minutes a day.
  3. Step 3

    Help friends or family first

    Whatever your niche, start by helping the people around you. Collect testimonials from these first clients and pin them to your highlights — this is what makes a stranger trust you.

  4. Step 4

    Give something free

    A free PDF or a free webinar builds even more trust with potential clients. Make a detailed plan that tells them everything they want to know.

    Don't hold anything back. Tell them everything you know from your own experience.

  5. Step 5

    Start selling

    REELSare where you gain attention.

    STORIESare where you sell.

Mini‑FAQ

The questions everyone asks before they start.

Do I need to be an "expert" to coach?

You need to be one or two steps ahead with a repeatable process. Start with beginner outcomes you can actually deliver.

What if I'm worried people will judge me?

Keep your content practical and professional: tips, checklists and simple frameworks. You don't need to share your whole life — share useful help.

Which platform should I choose?

Pick the one you'll use consistently for 30 days. If you like quick videos, choose TikTok. If you like text and networking, choose LinkedIn. If you like community and DMs, choose Instagram.

How do I get clients with a small following?

You don't need followers — you need conversations. Start with DMs, research chats, and referrals from people who already know you.

When can I reduce hours or quit?

When you have consistent paying clients and a runway. A common target is 3 months of steady revenue plus savings before you reduce shifts or leave.
